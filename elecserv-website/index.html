NYC

Elecserv Electrical Testing Co. is a minority-owned, technician-led electrical testing company made up of New York City locals with decades of experience in the region's unique and energy-dense electrical infrastructure.

From commissioning and acceptance testing to medium- and high-voltage splicing, substation maintenance and power-system studies, the team is focused on quality, safety, technical accuracy and the customer's success.
